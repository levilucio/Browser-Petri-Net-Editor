\section{Existing evidence and planned evaluation}
\subsection{Evidence provenance}
The scientific foundation note and audit are pinned at
\code{34b6078e5fea} on a separate documentation branch~\cite{audit}. They inspected
increment 38 at \code{1fa8b65e2ef8}, not B0. The original campaign artifact is dated
19 October 2026; the audit reports a fresh replay matching all 240 run identities
and verdicts. Increment 39 changes GUI execution transport; inspected SATEL/APN
semantic-core files are unchanged between these two implementation baselines.
This observation is not a new campaign rerun on B0.

The campaign uses three procedural families, eight variants including three
baselines, and five deliberately faulty variants. They are architecturally separate
from the APN simulator but were not built under a blinded independent-development
protocol. Aggregate joint outcomes are:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
History outcome & HML witnessed & HML counterexample & HML inconclusive\\\midrule
Compatible & 0 & 0 & 160\\
Mismatch & 0 & 16 & 64\\\midrule
Total & 0 & 16 & 224\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
There are 80 mismatch executions, not 80 distinct bugs. All five faulty variants
produce at least one mismatch. The 16 counterexamples refute one forbidden-output
property on one faulty variant. No complete selected property is witnessed in
these campaigns, and 224 inconclusive outcomes are not satisfactions.

Path denominators matter. Worker campaigns contain 16 open-only paths and 16 paths
that also exercise completion. On completion paths the failed-worker target yields
9/16 mismatches and the wrong-receipt target 16/16. The Choice target's seven
mismatches occur on 16 open-left paths; its 16 open-right paths remain compatible.
These designed fixtures and deterministic seed schedules do not support
population-level detection rates or probabilistic confidence intervals.

The audit also reports 2,480 checks against independent exact truth on a small
synthetic LTS corpus: 332 definite results and no definite-value disagreement.
This bounded check supports the evidence rules but does not prove P2. Increment
39 separately reports 750 unit/integration checks and 46 browser checks in each
of development and built modes~\cite{increment39}. Those are engineering regression
results with different scope, not additional black-box campaign executions.

\subsection{Next experiments, not yet results}
\paragraph{X2: independent evidence-rule checking.}
Enumerate tiny finite LTSs and bounded formulas across the implemented operators,
observations and complements. Compare every definite evidence value with an
independent exact evaluator, using both required polarities. Include refusals,
private predicates, timeouts and cutoffs. Retain corpus dimensions, unknown counts
and minimized counterexamples; do not report only a zero-disagreement headline.

\paragraph{X3: controlled selection ablation.}
Use the existing worker reference and targets, fixed completion-capable paths and
identical seeds. Compare legal sequence-only selection (for example amount 1) with
joint sequence/HML selection (amount 3), retaining open-only paths as a separate
control. Measure admission and per-path mismatch/evidence outcomes. The expected
difference is currently a source-based hypothesis, not a measured result.

\paragraph{Optional transfer study.}
If independent software applicability is central, construct one synchronous service
from an API/requirements contract with a recorded development protocol, frozen
tests and a fault ledger. Include an intentionally unobservable or insufficient-depth
fault. Do not introduce HTTP, asynchronous behavior or larger benchmarks solely
to enlarge the implementation before the semantic claims are settled.
