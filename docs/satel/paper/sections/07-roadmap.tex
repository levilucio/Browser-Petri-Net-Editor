\section{Threats, claim boundaries and writing roadmap}
Passing admission establishes agreement with the implemented reference evaluator,
not a proof of that evaluator or of the thesis correspondence. Shared errors
between symbolic encoding and concrete semantics are possible. Independent tiny-LTS
checking is useful for modal evidence, but does not independently validate all APN
algebraic operations. Budget exhaustion and incomplete unfolding prevent exhaustive
selection claims. Disjoint regions alone do not establish coverage or justify a
uniformity hypothesis.

The adapter abstraction and reset contract are assumptions, not outcomes of type
validation. Hidden state, output projection and seed interpretation constrain what
can be concluded. Finite compatible histories do not establish bisimulation, and
sampling does not recover state identity without a stronger control contract.
The current evaluator is deliberately conservative rather than maximally decisive.
Related-work comparison, development independence and operational sampling remain
separate limitations on publication claims.

thesis interpretation issues, and develop P1/P2 with X2. Then add X3 to isolate
what joint branching selection contributes. A restricted P5 correspondence result
can be added once its algebra and state/label translation are explicit. Keep
completeness, reduction-family equality and statistical guarantees outside the
abstract until established. Further optimization is justified only by a named
experiment blocked at its declared budget or a demonstrated GUI review problem.
